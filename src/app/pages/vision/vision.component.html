Our Story

Small businesses are the backbone of every community. They provide personalized service, create local jobs, and build lasting relationships with customers. Yet, despite their expertise, many struggle with one common challenge: attracting a steady flow of new customers while efficiently managing their schedules.

Every day, millions of appointment slots go unfilled because businesses lack the technology, marketing infrastructure, automation, or online visibility that larger organizations enjoy.

AppointmentBK was created to change that.

Founded by Mehul Vaidya and Hardik Chokshi, AppointmentBK combines deep expertise in healthcare operations, customer experience, software engineering, cloud technologies, scalable systems, and digital automation to build a platform that empowers service-based businesses to compete and thrive in the modern digital economy.

We believe technology should not be reserved for enterprise organizations. It should be simple, affordable, intelligent, and accessible to every entrepreneur.

Our mission is bigger than scheduling appointments.

Our mission is helping businesses grow.

Our Mission

To empower service-based businesses with intelligent technology that attracts new customers, automates appointment booking, maximizes schedule utilization, and creates sustainable business growth.
