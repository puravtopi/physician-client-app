Introduction

Appointment Booking, Inc. (“Appointment Booking,” “Company,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to explaining how personal information may be collected, used, disclosed, and protected when you access or use our website and services.

Appointment Booking, Inc. is a Michigan corporation established in June 2026 and based in Livonia, Michigan.

This Privacy Policy applies to the website currently located at www.apptbk.com. It also applies to any associated websites, applications, appointment-booking tools, provider directories, lead-generation features, account services, and other online services that link to this Privacy Policy (collectively, the “Platform” or “Services”), unless a separate privacy notice applies.

Our Platform is intended to help individuals discover service providers, submit inquiries, request or schedule appointments, and connect with participating businesses or professionals. Depending on the features made available, the Platform may also allow providers to manage profiles, receive inquiries, manage appointment requests, and use related business tools.

This Privacy Policy explains:

  • How information may be retained and protected.
  • The choices and privacy rights that may be available to you.
  • How to contact us about privacy matters.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have had the opportunity to review this notice. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will seek that consent separately.

  • Essential operations: Supporting login sessions, account security, navigation, and core Platform functionality.

Third-party analytics, advertising, and tracking providers, if any, are to be determined. We will identify relevant providers or update this notice when those arrangements are finalized and disclosure is required.
